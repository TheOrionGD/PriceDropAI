8. Full Description
PriceDrop AI is a smart, data-driven shopping assistant and price comparison tracker designed to help shoppers discover the best prices and save money.

Key Highlights:

1. Multi-Store Price Comparison:
Search for products or paste e-commerce links to instantly compare live verified offers across Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho, and Myntra.

2. Automated Price Drop Alerts:
Set target price thresholds on any tracked product. The application monitors prices in the background using battery-friendly Android WorkManager and alerts you instantly when prices drop to or below your target.

3. Verified Price History Charts:
Inspect genuine price snapshots recorded over time. Explore 7-day, 30-day, and full price history trends with an interactive canvas chart.

4. AI Shopping Copilot:
Ask the built-in AI Copilot to analyze real product offers, determine which store offers the lowest price, evaluate whether to buy now or wait, and review customer feedback pros and cons.

5. Bank & Payment Discount Calculator:
View instant discounts, cashback offers, and capped percentage savings on popular payment methods.

6. Offline-First Architecture:
All tracked items, price histories, and recent product searches are stored locally in a Room database, ensuring fast access even without an active internet connection.

9. Main Features
- Live multi-store search and price comparison across Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, and Myntra
- Background price drop monitoring with Android notification channels
- Customizable target price alerts with duplicate notification prevention
- Interactive historical price chart with touch inspection and timeframe filtering
- AI shopping copilot for product evaluation and price trend analysis
- Payment offer and bank discount calculator
- Clean URL parsing and share intent handling from external browsers and apps
- Offline-first cache backed by Jetpack Room
- Modern Material 3 user interface with full dark mode support
