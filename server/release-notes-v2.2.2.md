## PriceDropAI v2.2.2 (Build 19)

### 🚀 What's New & Enhancements in this Release

- **AI Review Summarization**:
  - Added Gemini-powered review synthesis directly into product details.
  - Generates pros, cons, sentiment breakdown, and consensus verdict.
  - Cached securely with fallback mechanisms.

- **Bank & Payment Card Offer Calculator**:
  - Added payment offer calculator supporting top credit/debit cards (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Amazon Pay ICICI).
  - Calculates instant discounts, cashbacks, and effective final price.
  - Supports custom user card selection in settings.

- **PriceDrop AI Copilot**:
  - Embedded conversational assistant for checking deals, price history analysis, and purchase timing recommendations.
  - Multi-store price comparison and deal authenticity score.

- **Android App & Build**:
  - Incremented `versionCode` to **19** and `versionName` to **2.2.2**.
  - Production signed release APK and App Bundle (AAB).
