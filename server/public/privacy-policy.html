Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how PriceDrop AI collects, uses, and protects your data.

Last Updated: October 2026

📌
1. Introduction & Overview

PriceDrop AI ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an intelligent shopping assistant designed to help users track e-commerce product prices, compare multi-store offers, calculate payment discounts, and receive background price drop notifications.

We are committed to maintaining an offline-first, user-privacy-centric approach. Your personal search habits, target price thresholds, and security credentials remain under your direct control on your device.

🛡️
2. Information We Collect & How It Is Stored

We believe in minimal data collection. Here is a detailed breakdown of the data types handled by the Application:

Data Category Data Types Collected Storage Location
Account & Profile Username & SHA-256 salted password hash Stored locally in Android SharedPreferences
App Activity & Trackers Product queries, target prices, watchlist items Stored locally in SQLite/Room Database (`pricedrop.db`)
Technical & API Data Standard HTTP headers (IP, User-Agent) during search Transient in-memory processing on backend API

Note on Passwords: We never store plain-text passwords. Passwords are salted and hashed locally using SHA-256 on your Android device for local profile access verification only.

🔗
3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

To provide real-time price comparisons and AI-assisted shopping insights, PriceDrop AI communicates with the following external services:

  • Backend Aggregation Server (`pricedropai-backend.onrender.com`): Handles live price scraping and search requests across Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho, and Myntra.
  • Google Gemini AI API: Powers the AI Shopping Copilot to evaluate live product deals, suggest whether to buy or wait, and analyze offer pros and cons.
  • Android Jetpack Libraries: Uses standard Android frameworks (Room, WorkManager, NotificationManager) for background price checks and local storage.

No personally identifiable information (PII) is sold, rented, or traded with any third-party advertising network.

⚙️
4. App Permissions & Usage Justification

PriceDrop AI requests only essential permissions required to deliver its core shopping utilities:

  • Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) to alert you when a product in your Watchlist drops to or below your custom target price.
  • Internet (`INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Required to fetch current product pricing from online stores and verify network connectivity before initiating background checks.

🗑️
5. Data Retention & User Data Deletion

Because PriceDrop AI utilizes an offline-first local database, you retain complete authority over your data:

  • Clear Search History: You can clear your recent search history directly from the search bar UI.
  • Remove Tracked Products: Items can be removed from your Watchlist at any time, instantly deleting them from local storage.
  • Account Logout & Wipe: Logging out clears the active local session and profile preferences.
  • Full App Reset: Clearing application storage via Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently erases all local databases, watchlist items, and saved credentials.

🔒
6. Security Measures

We enforce modern security best practices including HTTPS/TLS encryption for all backend communications, salted SHA-256 local hash storage for user accounts, and battery-optimized background WorkManager execution.

👶
7. Children's Privacy

PriceDrop AI is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us for immediate removal.
