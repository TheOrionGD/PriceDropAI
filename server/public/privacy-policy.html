Privacy Policy

Your privacy is central to how we build. Learn how PriceDrop AI collects, uses, and safeguards your information across mobile app and web services.

📅 Last Updated: October 26, 2026 (Version 2.2.0)

📌
1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

PriceDrop AI ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an intelligent, offline-first shopping assistant that tracks multi-platform e-commerce prices, provides instant deal comparisons, offers AI shopping advice, and notifies users of price drops.

We are firmly committed to an offline-first, privacy-centric design. Your shopping habits, watchlist tracking thresholds, saved items, and account credentials remain stored locally on your physical device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

🛍️
2. Multi-Store Search & Platform Coverage

PriceDrop AI aggregates public pricing and stock information across 14 major retail platforms:

Amazon India Flipkart Meesho Myntra Croma Reliance Digital JioMart Tata CLiQ Ajio Nykaa Blinkit eBay Walmart Target

Product Category Filtering: Platforms that do not carry a searched product category (e.g., fashion-only platforms searched for electronics, or tech stores searched for cosmetics) display price ₹0 with a transparent (Product Unavailable..) badge and are automatically excluded from lowest-price calculations.

Fair Use Search Limit: Because PriceDrop AI runs on free-tier community cloud infrastructure, we recommend searching 2 to 3 products at a time to prevent server delays and maintain optimal response times for everyone.

🛡️
3. Information We Collect & Storage Architecture

We practice strict data minimization. Here is exactly what data is handled and where it resides:

Data Category Data Elements Storage Location Purpose
Account & Profile Username & SHA-256 salted password hash Locally on device (Android SharedPreferences) Local profile authentication & access control
Watchlist & Trackers Product IDs, titles, target prices, store links Locally on device (Room SQLite: `pricedrop.db`) Price tracking & automated threshold comparisons
Notifications & Alerts Price drop notifications, in-app activity logs Locally on device (`pricedrop.db`) In-app Notification Center and system notification history
Search Queries Product keyword text (e.g., "running shoes") Transient in-memory on backend API Executing live price comparisons across 14 stores

Password Security: We never transmit or store plain-text passwords. All credentials are salted and hashed on your Android device using SHA-256 encryption.

No Cloud Profiling: We do not associate your search queries with your real-world identity, email, or advertising identifiers.

🤖
4. AI Copilot & Third-Party Integrations

To provide accurate shopping advice, price drop predictions, and real-time deal analysis, PriceDrop AI interfaces with the following service providers:

  • Groq AI LPU Models: High-speed language inference (including openai/gpt-oss-120b, openai/gpt-oss-20b, and qwen/qwen3.8-27b) to answer conversational shopping queries with low latency.
  • Google Gemini AI API: Fallback intelligent reasoning models (gemini-2.0-flash, gemini-1.5-flash) to synthesize buyer reviews, pros & cons, and market trends.
  • PriceDrop Backend Gateway: Proxies store searches securely over HTTPS/TLS to protect client devices from direct web scraping overhead.
  • Android Jetpack Architecture: Uses official Google Android libraries (Room, WorkManager, NotificationManager) for battery-efficient background price checks.

No user identifiers, phone numbers, or personal accounts are ever sent to AI model providers. Only the public product name and search query are processed.

⚙️
5. Android App Permissions & Purpose

PriceDrop AI requests minimal permissions strictly needed for functionality:

  • System Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) to dispatch phone-level system notifications when a tracked product drops below your chosen target price or when in-app activity messages are delivered.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Allows optional haptic alerts when an urgent price drop alert fires. Can be customized in Settings.
  • Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to fetch live store pricing and check connectivity before background price checks execute.

🗑️
7. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because PriceDrop AI is offline-first, you maintain full control over your stored data:

  • Clear Search History: Tap the "Clear" button in the search bar to erase all recent queries.
  • Delete Tracked Products: Swipe or tap delete on any product in your Watchlist to permanently remove it from local storage.
  • Clear Notifications: Tap "Clear All" in the Notifications Center to erase saved price drop logs.
  • Account Logout & Data Wipe: Logging out resets the session; clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local databases.

PriceDrop AI is a general shopping utility intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe such data has been collected, please contact us for immediate deletion.
